Our Story

North Star Bakery started as a small stand at the neighborhood farmers market. We had one oven and a folding table. People kept asking for more bread and more pastries, so we opened a real store. Our bakers still show up before the sun comes up to mix and shape everything by hand.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We try to use flour from local mills. We use fruit and herbs that are in season. We do not use shortcuts or artificial ingredients. We just use flour, water, salt, and time.

Meet the Team

Maria Alvarez is our head baker and founder. She started the bakery at the farmers market and still opens the ovens most mornings.

Devon Price is our pastry chef. Devon plans the seasonal pastries and makes all of our custom cakes.

Sam Okafor runs the front of the store. Sam knows a lot of our customers by name and keeps our pre-orders organized.
